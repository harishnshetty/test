What are Labels and Selectors in Kubernetes?
A-Labels are key-value pairs attached to Kubernetes objects (e.g., Pods, Services) for identification and
grouping. Selectors are used to filter and select objects based on labels.


What is the difference between a StatefulSet and a Deployment in Kubernetes?
A- A StatefulSet is used for stateful applications that require stable, unique network identifiers and
persistent storage. It manages Pods that are not interchangeable, whereas a Deployment is used for
stateless applications and manages interchangeable Pods.
